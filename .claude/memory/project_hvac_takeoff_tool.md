Building an AI-powered HVAC takeoff tool similar to withrebar.ai.

**Why:** User's company provided the 50 files Rebar bootstrapped with. They have domain expertise, a human takeoff team, and want to build their own solution rather than depend on Rebar.

**How to apply:** 
- Phase-by-phase approach, internal tool first
- The human team is the key asset — they generate training data and validate accuracy
- Start with template matching (OpenCV) before graduating to deep learning
- Every design decision should account for long-term scalability to public product
